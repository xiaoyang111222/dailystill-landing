Last updated: September 18, 2026 · Brand: DailyStill

Information we collect

How we use information

  • To deliver digital products and send download / receipt emails

Sharing

Payment, tax, and checkout are handled by our Merchant of Record (Creem). We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with providers needed to process payments, host the site, or deliver downloads, or when required by law.

Data retention

Order and support records are kept as long as needed for delivery, accounting, dispute handling, and legal requirements.

Your choices

Contact us to request access or deletion of support correspondence we control, subject to legal retention needs. Payment-provider records may need to be requested from them directly.

Children
